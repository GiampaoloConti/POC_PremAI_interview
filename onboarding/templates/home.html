How a file moves

  1. The client registers and sees which three documents are needed. They send them in any order, whenever they have them.
  2. The agent reads each document as it arrives, tells the client what is still missing and sends reminders.
  3. The agent drafts the bank form. Every answer quotes its source. What is not in the documents is left empty, never guessed.
  4. The employee reviews only the answers that need a person, approves the form and sends it to the bank.
  5. The bank replies. The agent reads the reply and prepares the next step. The employee confirms.
